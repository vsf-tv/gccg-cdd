# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in this VSF Technical Recommendation.

VSF Technical Recommendations and other work products are developed and ratified in accordance with 
the [VSF Document Procedures](https://vsf.tv/policies/VSF_Document_Process_Policy.pdf).  They are 
created and updated in VSF Activity Groups, requiring VSF membership for participation.

VSF member contributions to this Technical Recommendation are made subject to VSF's [IPR Policy](https://vsf.tv/policies/vsf-ipr-policy_v3.pdf). 
Contributions should be accompanied by the form in an appendix of that policy.

For more information for those wishing to develop or contribute to VSF Technical Recommendations,
please contact a chair of the relevant [VSF Activity Group](https://vsf.tv/activity_groups.shtml).

## Reporting Issues

VSF members and non-members are welcome to raise issues via the GitHub Issues tab.

## Pull Requests

We accept Pull Requests from VSF members participating in relevant workgroups. These are subject to the IPR Policy referenced above.

Each VSF Activity Group has a number of members that will review a PR.

## Suggesting Improvements or Enhancements

We are interested in hearing of suggestions, both for the spec itself and the accompanying documentation. 
Please contact the Activity Group chair or a [VSF Board Member](https://vsf.tv/officers_directors.shtml).
